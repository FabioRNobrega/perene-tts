Perene TTS for Apple-silicon Macs (M1 or later, macOS 14 Sonoma or later)

1. Double-click the zip if it is not unpacked yet, then drag "Perene TTS" into
   your Applications folder.
2. Open Applications and double-click "Perene TTS".
3. The first time, macOS says it cannot verify the app, because it is not
   notarized by Apple. Click "Done" (not "Move to Trash").
4. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
   "Open Anyway" next to the Perene TTS message. Confirm with your password.
5. Perene TTS opens. Later launches open directly.

Terminal alternative (instead of steps 3-4):
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Perene TTS.app"

Everything runs on this Mac, offline. Your voices, creations and batches are
kept in ~/Library/Application Support/Perene TTS and survive app updates.
